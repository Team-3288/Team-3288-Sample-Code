Don't touch Telemetry.java, CommandSwerveDrivetrain.java, or TunerConstants.java by hand those are the main files that makes swerve drive work with CTRE. If you ever need to change the modules offset use the CTRE app and do it that way

Main.java and Robot.java are off limits they are the MAIN files that run the bot if you mess with them you might break your code and bot.
